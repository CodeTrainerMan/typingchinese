## 二、产品介绍（Description）

```markdown
Learn Chinese one keystroke at a time — type the pinyin, hear the tones, and keep the word.

TypingChinese is a clean, modern, ad-free web tool designed for Chinese learners to practice vocabulary and typing without distractions.

Key Features:
- ⌨️ 4 Practice Modes: Follow-typing, dictation, self-test, and write-from-meaning.
- 🎯 Spaced Repetition (FSRS): Missed words are automatically scheduled for scientific review.
- 📚 Full HSK 1–6 Vocabulary: Over 5,000 words with tone-marked pinyin, audio, and translations.
- ✍️ Stroke Order Animations: Learn to write characters stroke-by-stroke in the interactive mi-grid.
- 🗂️ Free Anki Decks: Download the complete 5,004-word HSK deck (.apkg) directly.
- 🔒 100% Client-Side & Private: No sign-up, no account, runs entirely in your browser.
- 🌐 14 Interface Languages.

100% Free and Open Source on GitHub!
```

## 三、Maker First Comment（发布后的第一条官方留言）

```markdown
Hi Product Hunt community! 👋

I'm William, the creator of TypingChinese.

When learning Chinese, I noticed a huge gap: traditional flashcard apps test passive recognition, but real communication requires active production — typing pinyin accurately and remembering the tones.

So I built TypingChinese: a completely free, open-source web app that turns vocabulary practice into an interactive typing experience. 

Why it's different:
1. Zero Friction: No forced accounts, no paywalls, no ads. All progress stays in your browser.
2. Scientific Review: Powered by the modern FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) algorithm.
3. Audio & Stroke Order: Native speech synthesis and animated character stroke orders.
4. Anki Companion: We even packaged all 5,004 HSK words into a free downloadable Anki deck.

I'd love to hear your feedback, feature ideas, or thoughts on language learning!

Happy typing! 🚀
```
